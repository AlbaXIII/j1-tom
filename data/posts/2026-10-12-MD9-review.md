With the bumper international break now behind us, the J League returned with a bang, with a bevy of drama, late goals, screamers and missed sitters to boot.

<br>

Resuming on Friday night, **Kashima Antlers** finally snapped their winless streak of 4 games with a hard-fought 2-0 win at home to **Gamba Osaka**, courtesy of Léo Ceará's 11th of the season and a first of the campaign for Yuma Suzuki. Gamba have now lost 4 in a row - rookie manager Tomukazu Myojin now has much riding on their next fixture away to Shimizu, as his side are now mired in the relegation zone. **Cerezo Osaka** and **Yokohama F. Marinos** played out a 0-0 stalemate, a result keeping both teams hovering in mid-table. It was almost a similar story at **Avispa Fukuoka** as they hosted **Fagiano Okayama** - with the game locked at 1-1 a deflected Yu Hashimoto strike undid all of Fagiano's hard work and giving the Wasps a 2-1 win and a route out of the relegation zone (at Gamba's expense). Quietly gaining momentum are **FC Tokyo** - who overcame **Urawa Reds** - despite an even first half, Riziko Matsuhashi's side put 3 unanswered strikes past Shusaku Nishikawa to go within 1 point of the leaders at the summit of the table. Level on points with them are **Machida Zelvia**, who were lucky not to go behind to **Kyoto Sanga** before an 86th minute Takehiro Akimoto winner keeps Machida in the hunt. Down at the other end of the table, **JEF United Chiba** opened the scoring almost immediately before wasting a host of chances and conceding a 92nd minute Kenshin Yasuda equalizer at home to **V-Varen Nagasaki** - JEF United will feel this a real opportunity lost to leave the relegation zone. **Mito Hollyhock** lost a 3rd game on the bounce, this time at home to **Shimizu S-Pulse** - the hosts went into the break 1-0 ahead but a raft of subs from Shimizu manager Takayuki Yoshida turned the game on it's head and his side ran out 2-1 winner, their 3rd in a row. No new manager bounce for **Tokyo Verdy**, who after parting company with Hiroshi Jofuku hosted **Sanfrecce Hiroshima**. New coach Naoki Soma could not work any magic right away though with a screamer from Taishi Matsumoto and an unfortunate own goal keeping Verdy rooted to the bottom - Sanfrecce stay in joint second, a point off the league leaders. Lastly, **Kawasaki Frontale** and **Nagoya Grampus** shared a frantic start with both sides scoring before the 5th minute. With the game settling down, it was the away side who came away with all 3 points thanks to Yuya Yamagishi's winner.

<br>

## GAME(S) OF THE ROUND

In Friday night's other fixture, it was a top-of-the-table clash between **Kashiwa Reysol** and **Vissel Kobe**. Kobe sat on the summit before the game, but were thoroughly well-beaten by an exuberant Reysol team, who edged an even first half before once again tearing away in the second. Ricardo Rodriguez' side are capable, on their day, of some of the most fluid and dynamic football in the division and Kobe could not live with them - Reysol were 3-0 up by the time Kobe could mount a response, and by then it was too late. Another excellent Reysol performance and a great result for the league, with Kashiwa leapfrogging Kobe into 1st and leaving 2 points between 1st and 5th. The title race is getting interesting already!
