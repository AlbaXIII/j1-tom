The J League produced another weekend of thrills and (a lot of) spills this weekend as the heavens opened all across Japan.

## GAME OF THE ROUND
The season is really beginning to take shape as we head towards the first international break, and fascinating stories are being written all over the league. Kashima ditched the heart-attack ball for a regulation win over Tokyo Verdy, keeping pace with Kashiwa who did the same to Shimizu S-Pulse. Machida Zelvia and Sanfrecce Hiroshima were both held – the former by the hugely improved Mito Hollyhock, the latter by 10-man Gamba Osaka. Vissel Kobe were once again unimpressive in a tight win over Cerezo Osaka, and Nagoya joined them on 6 points by snatching all 3 points against Fagiano Okayama at the death. At the sharp end, V-Varen Nagasaki lost their third on the bounce to a resurgent FC Tokyo, and Kyoto Sanga took all 3 points against Avispa Fukuoka. However, there were only 2 real competitors for the showpiece of the round. Kawasaki Frontale’s 4-2 win against poor old JEF United Chiba was chaotic, often slapstick, riotous fun but for me the game of the weekend was **Urawa 3-2 Yokohama F. Marinos**.  The Red Diamonds deservedly collecting their first points of the season in a barnstorming seesaw contest to ease the pressure on new boss Gwi-jae Jo.

## PLAYER OF THE ROUND
It is time once again to give **Léo Ceará** his flowers. His 5th and 6th league goals of the season were enough to seal a thankfully routine away win for Kashima Antler’s over the ever-obstinate Tokyo Verdy. Ceará has made the outstanding the norm, scoring in every game this season. In a competition against a side as stubbornly rigid as Verdy, he has the spark, movement, creativity and most of all clinical finishing to win games on his own - it would take a brave person to bet against another Ceará golden boot.
