Red cards and last minute winners galore as the curtain comes down on the first round of J1 League games before the international break.

<br>

Going into the window on top remain **Vissel Kobe**, who took till the 90th minute to break the resolve of a surprisingly gritty **Gamba Osaka**, with the goal courtesy of half-time substitute Erik to grab the 0-1 win. Amongst the chasing pack, **Sanfrecce Hiroshima** similarly had to grind out a 0-1 away win at **Avispa Fukuoka**, who had a man sent off in each half and still almost nicked a point, in no small part due to the performance of goalkeeper Yuma Obata - sadly for the Wasps, they are rooted in the relegation zone and are winless in 5. **FC Tokyo** remain in touching distance of the top 3 on goal difference after a sublime individual goal by Kota Tawaratsumida was enough to beat **Nagoya Grampus** - although they also had to see the game out with 10 men. Heading in to mid-table, **Yokohama F. Marinos** unbeaten run now stands at 4 games as they cruised to a 2-0 home win over **Mito Hollyhock**, who now have now lost 2 on the spin. A daft red card for forward Lucão with the score locked at 1-1 meant that **Fagiano Okayama** surrended the points 1-2 to the visiting **Kyoto Sanga**, who broke a 3-game winless run. **Shimizu S-Pulse** enjoyed a routine enough home win over **JEF United Chiba**, the 2-1 victory leaving the visitors bottom at the break, whilst **V-Varen Nagasaki** and **Cerezo Osaka** shared the spoils in Nagasaki 1-1, although the hosts will think they deserved more. Joining JEF United in the drop zone are the hapless **Tokyo Verdy**, who got turned over 3-1 at **Urawa Reds** with the damage all being done in the first half.

<br> 

## GAME(S) OF THE ROUND

It is very hard to separate the contenders for this weeks showpiece game, so they both are very worth winners. Few fans would have predicted that **Kashima Antlers** would lose 3 on the bounce all season, let alone this early, so the trip to **Kawasaki Frontale** took on huge importance - with Frontale winning 2 of their last 3 and looking very upwardly mobile. What followed was a see-saw game of fabulous goals, 6 goals shared apiece, with both teams going blow for blow until the 97th minute when Pedro Romano grabbed a deserved equaliser for the home side.

<br>

The other game of the week was the top of the table clash between (in my opinion) the most impressive sides so far this season - **Machida Zelvia** and **Kashiwa Reysol**. Machida were still the only unbeaten team left in the league even though they had slipped behind Kobe into 2nd whilst Kashiwa had recovered from 2 losses on the bounce and were back in full flow, with an astonishing comeback victory last week to build on, and build they did. As with last week, Reysol went into half time 1-0 down, but whatever Ricardo Rodriguez is saying to them at half time is rocket fuel - they flew out of the blocks in the second half _again_ and tore into Zelvia, scoring 3 in 12 minutes with _another_ double for Yusuke Segawa. Ultimately winning 4-2 in the pouring rain (aided by a red card for Zelvia's Hokuto Shimoda), Kashiwa are up to 2nd, Machida slip to 4th, and it's all very much to play for with 2 points between the entire top 5. More of the same when we come back please!!
