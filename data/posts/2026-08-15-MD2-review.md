Roll up, roll up - it's time for another Matchday review.

## GAME OF THE ROUND

Whilst not as explosive as last week, there were still some standout games this weekend. Sanfrecce & Machida Zelvia handed out some beatings to Urawa and JEF United, whilst there were a few very entertaining draws between Vissel - FC Tokyo and Kawasaki - Kyoto. Kashima Antlers also underlined their resilience (again) by winning in 90"+ squeaky-bum time. However, there must be something about those Friday night lights because my vote goes to **Tokyo Verdy 1-3 Kashiwa Reysol**. As much as it pains me to watch Verdy lose, on their day Reysol are an absolute joy to watch and Ricardo Rodriguez' team cut Verdy to bits at times, with all three goals being of high technical quality. Verdy were obstinate as ever but ultimately not good enough to compete, and they won't be the first to feel that way after facing Kashiwa this season.

## GOAL OF THE ROUND

Difficult choice this week, with there being some excellent strikes all around the league. [**Tojiro Kubo**](https://youtu.be/FA4njaP9c5U?si=QqShaQJ2PQRjdh1H&t=482) finished off a flowing Reysol move, [**Lucao**](https://youtu.be/riQYmifqWt0?si=L40x7v5l13tgTnx7&t=288) finished a one-on-one like it owed him money and [**Yuta Nakayama**](https://youtu.be/ospHXLO_1EI?si=Hk-ZEldGyKJrZwEv) stroked home the first direct free kick of the season. Sometimes though, you can't beat a good welly, and **Takuma Nishimura** obliged with a thunderbolt against poor JEF United. Foot like a traction engine etc.

## SAVE OF THE ROUND

Another stacked field this week, with quite a few outstanding saves across the board. First among them was  **Ruben Blanco**, who had multiple point-blank stops of pure reflex, effectively sealing the points for Yokohama F. Marinos as well as a very well deserved clean sheet.

## PLAYER OF THE ROUND

Somewhat a running theme this week is the huge array of competitors for every award. **Akito Suzuki** was outstanding during Sanfrecce's resounding win, the aforementioned **Yuta Nakayama** was again a hugely effective threat for Machida Zelvia and **Léo Ceará** did Léo Ceará things. However I'm going to go for an unsung hero this week with Kyoto Sanga's **Shinosuke Fukuda**. A sublime finish and pinpoint assist, not a bad day's work for a right-back.
