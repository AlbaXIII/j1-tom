The highlights and lowlights of another exceptional weekend in the J1 league.

## GAME OF THE ROUND

Enthralling games abound once again in the J1 league the weekend. Highlights inclued Mito Hollyhock securing their first win in J1 this century in superb style beating Kyoto Sanga 1-3 in their own patch, Kashima Antlers riding out a chaotic win (again), this time 3-2 against the unlucky Avispa Fukuoka and another excellent Machida Zelvia performance cruising to a 3-1 win over the Urawa Red Diamonds. However, I worry slightly that this site will eventually become a Kashiwa Reysol fanclub but the game of the weekend was the Taiyō-Ō defeating a game yet ultimately outclassed V-Varen Nagasaki 4-2 on Friday night. Reysol were again brilliant to watch and the game as a competition was enhanced hugely by the visitors who gave it a real go - even taking the lead in the second half - but the early frontrunners again had too much for the opposition. I might have to cut and paste this as the season goes on.

## GOAL OF THE ROUND

It's going to become a question of catch him if you can with **Shin Miidera**. The 16-year-old bagged his second of the season against Vissel Kobe, and it was a beaut to boot - a perfectly executed left-footed volley to win his side all 3 points. His is a name that might not be in Japan for much longer, as the eyes of the football elite fall upon him after a blistering start to the season. Honourable mentions to [Masahito Ono](https://youtu.be/L2rmD77WVrs?t=548) curling one in off the post with one of the crispest post sounds I've heard in a minute, and [Yudai Kimura](https://youtu.be/L2rmD77WVrs?t=14) cutting the grass from a mile out - both for Nagoya in their 3-1 win over Gamba Osaka.

## PLAYER OF THE ROUND

Kawasaki Frontale had the unenviable task of heading to Sanfrecce Hiroshima this matchday to face down Bartosch Gaul's freewheeling league traiblazers. Sanfrecce had won their fist 2 games by an aggregate score of 7-1, so few would have given Kawasaki a chance in this one, and yet they gave as good as they got in a battling 1-1 draw. A key reason for this being the performance of **Yuki Yamamoto** in midfield - he was everywhere, driving the team on, making tackles - and grabbed himself a goal to cap it all off.
