A slightly delayed roundup this week for matchday 7 (a sad consequence of moving house), but this round of games was no less exciting and impactful as usual in the best league in the east. Stories abound at both ends of the table, 100th minute winners, crazy comebacks, the whole nine yards. 

<br>

Starting at the top of the table - taple-toppers **Machida Zelvia** had their momentum clipped in a 1-1 home draw with a grimly determined **Yokohama F. Marinos**. The visitors took the lead early on, and soaked up wave after wave of Machida pressure - who also went down to 10 men  midway through - during the second half only to have their resistance broken in the 84th minute. Indeed Machida were lucky to come away with a point, as Kaina Tanimura was denied a second for the Marinos by a (very) lengthy VAR check and they also rattled the bar in the last minute. The beneficiaries in the other clash at the top were **Vissel Kobe**, who overcame a severely wobbling **Kashima Antlers** 2-1 - a Léo Ceará-less Kashima are now on 3 consecutive losses in the league, with Kobe closing the gap on Machida to 1 point. Elsewhere in the lofty heights of the table, **FC Tokyo** visited **Gamba Osaka** and ran out very convincing 0-2 winners. Gamba are really struggling - now without a win since the opening game, whilst FC Tokyo are unbeaten in 5. In the late game on Sunday, **Fagiano Okayama** ended the mini-revival of **Urawa Reds** by snatching a 1-2 win with a winner in the 100th minute. **Shimizu S-Pulse** finally got back to winning ways, 1-0 over the listless **Avispa Fukuoka**, and **Kawasaki Frontale** went to **Mito Hollyhock** and ended their unbeaten run by the same score, despite Mito keeper Konosuke Nishikawa almost holding out on his own. **V-Varen Nagasaki** got their second win of the season by deservedly turning **Nagoya Grampus** over at home 2-0, Brazilians Matheus Jesus and Thiago Santana with the strikes. In the big basement battle of the day, it was a tale of 2 goalkeeper mistakes as **Tokyo Verdy** and **JEF United Chiba** shared a 1-1 draw - JEF thought they had the day but a late howler from Jose Suarez (not the first this season) gave Verdy a share of the points, which helps basically neither side - they remain rooted in the bottom 2 spots, 1 point from safety.

<br>

## GAME OF THE ROUND

Which leaves us with two options for game of the day, and as much as I enjoyed the absolute shellacking **Sanfrecce Hiroshima** dished out to **Cerezo Osaka** - 6-1, 2 penalties, and some truly brutally efficient counter-attacking exemplified by Shun Ayukawa's hat-trick - I have to give it to **Kyoto Sanga 2-3 Kashiwa Reysol**. Reysol came into the game on the back of 2 defeats, and when they went 2-0 down at half time - courtesy of the consistently outstanding Rafael Elias - few would have thought that streak would end. But in the second half, Reysol fought their way back into the game and in a 12-minute flurry completed the turnaround. It was stirring stuff, and a message to the league that they are very much ready to fight for this title.
