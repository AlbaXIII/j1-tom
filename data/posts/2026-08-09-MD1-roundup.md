And we are back! 10 games, 35 goals, and standard J league madness to spare. Read on for my weekly roundup of all the highlights from the first matchday of the 2026/2027 season, starting with...

## GAME OF THE ROUND

You could choose either of the Friday night curtain-raisers, which saw 14 goals shared between them, but I have to give it to **Yokohama F. Marinos 3-4 Kashima Antlers**. 3-1 down heading in to the last 10 minutes, Kashima's comeback in the finale of this match was nothing less than bananas, featuring a disallowed equaliser, a "penalty" for handball that could only have been avoided with amputation, and a winner in the 102nd minute with Aleksandar Čavrić slipping it through the keepers legs to end an utterly barmy game of football. Is it too early to call this a sign of champions? Obviously it is, but it does appear that the Antlers are going to take some beating again this season.

## SKILL OF THE ROUND

Spare a thought for 16-year-old **Shiin Miidera** of Yokohama F. Marinos. To get both a goal and assist on your first ever J1 League match is incredibly impressive, and in some style too - the undisputed highlight being this first time heel flick over an Antlers captain Ueda's head from a huge clearance to set up the break to put Marinos 2-1 ahead. Shame they couldn't stick the landing but we should all watch his career with great interest.
