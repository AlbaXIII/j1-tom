It’s that time of year again! Where I expose that I actually know nothing about any of this by doing by best to predict the future in the form of my predicted 2026/2027 final J1 League table. Read on to see how wrong/right I am!

-

## **[Avispa Fukuoka](/team/1)**
The *Hornets* have been busy in the transfer market, with the signings of Oriol Romeu and Keiya Shiihashi designed to shore up a porous midfield that had become a weakness during the 100 Year Vision League. Should have enough to avoid relegation.
### Predicted Finish - **16th**

-

## **[Cerezo Osaka](/team/2)**
Cerezo had an excellent campaign during the special season and were well worth their 3rd place finish. Losing manager Arthur Papas to the Saudi Pro League is a big blow however, and though Pablo Machín has experience at the highest level in Europe he might take time to bed into the J League. Coupled with a few notable departures in the market, the tangible  progress that had been made might be held up this season.
### Predicted Finish - **9th**

-

## **[Fagiano Okayama](/team/3)**
Mid-table beckons again for Okayama, which would be about par for where they are at the moment. The signing of Song-ho Na from Machida Zelvia is a canny move which could propel them towards the top end of the table but I imagine another season of consolidation for *Fagi*.
### Predicted Finish - **11th**

-

## **[Gamba Osaka](/team/4)**
The potential is there for Gamba to bother the teams at the zenith of the table but there is also the potential for stagnation. Many questions to be answered: How will club legend Tomukazu Myojin get on in his first gig as manager? Will Deniz Hümmet carry on his blistering form from the Vision League? Can Takashi Usami keep up his ageless performances at 34? I’m going to wimp out and go straight down the middle.
### Predicted Finish - **10th**

-

## **[JEF United Chiba](/team/5)**
The first of the newly promoted sides to feature, JEF United had a rude introduction to J1 League life during the Vision League, finishing bottom overall by some distance. This clearly struck a chord with the club as the transfers being made are very interesting indeed – Erison from Kawasaki Frontale is a proven goalscorer at this level, and both Leonardo Rocha and Matheus Índio have top-level European experience. Will it be enough to stave off the drop – no, but it will be a much more competitive showing this time around. Don’t be surprised if they shock a few big beasts this season.
### Predicted Finish - **18th (Relegated)**

-

## **[Kashima Antlers](/team/6)**
The champions of the previous J League have a settled squad, the best attacker in the league in Léo Ceará, and have had no huge upheaval in the summer (indeed have probably strengthened with the arrival of Yan Matheus, despite losing Kimito Nono to D.C. United). My tips to retain their title.
### Predicted Finish - **Champions**

-

## **[Kashiwa Reysol](/team/7)**
The Vision League was a marked step backwards for last season’s runners up. Ricardo Rodriguez’ team looked a shadow of the team that pushed Kashima all the way, but this summer they look to have addressed a number of weaknesses in the squad – with a number of proven J League performers coming in such as Makoto Mitsuta, Keita Endo, and Shogo Asada - this proactivity in the market has softened the setback of losing Ota Yamamato to Europe. On their day Reysol are brilliant to watch and whilst I don’t think they will be in the title mix again, I think they will be back amongst the stronger sides in the division.
### Predicted Finish - **5th**

-

## **[Kawasaki Frontale](/team/8)**
Frontale are in a good position to kick on despite the loss of Erison to JEF United, and like the hydra, lose one Brazilian, three more will arrive in his place – Pedro Romano, Derik Lacerda and Kayke Queiroz had traveled the well-trodden path from the Série A to J1. It is debatable how much progress can be made in Kawasaki this year, but I envisage another strong season.
### Predicted Finish - **7th**

-

## **[Machida Zelvia](/team/9)**
Something seems to be brewing in Zelvia – and they are my outside shouts to be in the mix with the rest of the big clubs. My reasons for this are simple – Go Kuroda has built a strong squad with a number of match-winners (Erik, Yūki Sōma) and a sense of momentum that has been present since their promotion in 2024. 
### Predicted Finish - **6th**

-

## **[Mito Hollyhock](/team/10)**
The next of our newly promoted clubs that I expect will sadly struggle once again. Only JEF United performed worse in the Vision League, and even then, Hollyhock had the most porous defence in the entire division. A slew of activity in the summer has ensued, but the turnover could be a hindrance as much as benefit – although it will be interesting to see if centre back Rei Ieizumi can make the step up from J2. Sadly my pick for the basement.
### Predicted Finish - **20th (Relegated)**

-

## **[Nagoya Grampus](/team/11)**
Nagoya enjoyed the Vision League after a torrid season last time around, with a noticeable upturn in form under new boss Mihailo Petrović. This momentum will carry them into the new season in good stead, but a lack of proactivity in the transfer market will leave a lot on the shoulders of Yuya Yamagishi and Yudai Kimura to continue their strong form from the special season. 
### Predicted Finish - **8th**

-

## **[Kyoto Sanga](/team/12)**
As with Kashiwa Reysol, the intermediary season was not kind to Kyoto and they suffered a severe drop in form from their 3rd place finish last time out. Has this been remedied in the off-season? It will be interesting to find out - Ranko Popović arrives as manager with a...mixed record in Japan and they have lost a few outstanding talents to Europe in the shape of Kazunari Kita (permanently this time) and Sota Kawasaki. New manager bounce may materialise but I think Sanga will have an average season.
### Predicted Finish - **13th**

-

## **[Sanfrecce Hiroshima](/team/13)**
I really like the look of Sanfrecce this season. Losing Michael Skibbe to Vissel Kobe was unplanned but they have gone like for like in the shape of fellow German Bartosch Gaul. Whilst this is only his second top-flight gig as manager, he has been working in the academy system of the evil empire of RB in Leipzig and has been instrumental in the development of a plethora of top-class talent in over the last few years – so the man has an eye for talent. The moves being made in the market look very canny – if Sebastien Haller is even half the player he was he will score 10+, Takuma Asano is a hero of Japanese Football and returns from Spain at only 32, and Takumu Kawamura was outstanding during his last stint in Hiroshima. My picks to run Kashima the closest.
### Predicted Finish - **2nd**

-

## **[Shimizu S-Pulse](/team/14)**
Owners of the J League’s best kits are in an interesting place on the eve of the season. A season of consolidation following promotion in 2024 was followed by a middling Vision League but they have made several canny moves in the transfer market – Ryo Germain from Hiroshima, Dieguinho from Brazil to replace the departing Zento Uno – so don’t be surprised to see upward mobility from S-Pa.
### Predicted Finish - **12th**

-

## **[FC Tokyo](/team/15)**
Losing young phenom Ryūnosuke Satō to Europe before he could ever make an impact for his parent club is surely one of the cruellest moves in this summer’s market, but despite that, FC Tokyo are a club on the rise. Nicolai Vallys is an interesting addition and brings a direct threat, Hayato Inamura looked a real talent before his career stalled at Celtic, and the pickups of Shion Homma and Hirokazu Ishihara from Urawa look smart. Coupled with a strong existing core, I think they will have another strong year.
### Predicted Finish - **4th**
-

## **[Tokyo Verdy](/team/16)**
Sadly I think this will be another season of struggle for my boys in green. Much will depend as always on Koki Morita – if he stays fit they should fend off relegation, but as with the last few seasons, it won’t be pretty. 
### Predicted Finish - **15th**

-

## **[Urawa Red Diamonds](/team/17)**
It’s a new day in Urawa - Cho Kwi-jae arrives as manager with a burgeoning reputation forged at Kyoto Sanga still intact despite their slide during the special season. However I with Urawa I get the impression of a club in transition, not quite ready to make a burst for the top half but with more than enough to be safe. Some smart buys in the market (Harumi Minamino from Gamba) and some losses (Homma and Ishihara to FC Tokyo) play into that feeling. Probably the team I will be most wrong about.
### Predicted Finish - **14th**

-

## **[V-varen Nagasaki](/team/18)**
The last of our newly promoted trio, Nagasaki also had a rough time of it in the Vision League, although the did manage to stay off the bottom in the west (courtesy of goal difference ahead of Fukuoka) but that might be the limit of their success back in the top flight. The Brazilian contingent in the squad (Matheus Jesus especially) are dangerous on their day but will the signings of Takuma Ominami and Kanta Doi be enough to sure up the defence? J2 beckons.
### Predicted Finish - **19th (Relegated)**

-

## **[Vissel Kobe](/team/19)**
Deposed champions Vissel are on the warpath. Victory in the Vision League final over the Antlers will give them hope that the crown is theirs to take once again, and former Hiroshima boss Michael Skibbe proved a very clever appointment, already with a trophy in his hands. Anderson Lopes returning from Singapore should give the attack a huge boost and Kota Watanabe should strengthen the midfield but I think they will be pipped to the post this time around by Hiroshima and Kashima. It will be very close.
### Predicted Finish - **3rd**

-

## **[Yokohama F. Marinos](/team/20)**
Resident chaos merchants Yokohama F. Marinos have had a summer of renewal - Steve Corica has flown in from Auckland FC to try and fix up one of the best attacks and worst defences in the country. Much will rely on the creativity of Jordy Croux to continue to provide the bullets to Kaina Tanumura – a partnership with real promise. With Kota Watanabe and Ren Kato out the door though this may be another crazy season for the Marinos, and I predict they will be looking over their shoulders.
### Predicted Finish - **17th**
