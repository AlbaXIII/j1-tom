## GAME OF THE ROUND
A very tough one to call this week as there were a bevy of great games this Wednesday, featuring late comebacks, huge upsets and chaotic finales. On the more sedate end of things, Vissel Kobe cruised to a standard issue 2-0 win over the increasingly doomed-looking Tokyo Verdy (5 games, 1 point, 1 goal scored – grim reading), whilst joining Verdy in the misery-hole are JEF United, who threw a point away to Okoyama to lose 1-2 in the  94th minute. Kyoto Sanga and Yokohama F. Marinos played out a 1-1 draw, as did Shimizu S-Pulse and FC Tokyo. Sanfrecce Hiroshima got back into the groove by rolling Nagoya Grampus over 3-0, and Urawa Reds continued their revival by coming from behind to defeat Avispa Fukuoka 3-2  with 2 goals in the last 10 minutes. V-Varen Nagasaki thought they had their second win of the season by scoring twice in the dying embers of the game but an absolutely stoppage time comeback by Gamba Osaka saw them take a point. The games of most consequence took place at the top of the table – Kashiwa Reysol slipped up away at Cerezo Osaka, beaten 2-0 by Solomon Sakuragawa’s double, and Machida Zelvia continued their unbeaten start by overturning a 1-0 deficit in the last 10 minutes to snatch all 3 points from Kawaski Frontale. That result was enough to send Machida top, courtesy of the game of the day. With an absolutely titanic performance from the best of the newly promoted sides, **Mito Hollyhock 4-2 Kashima Antlers** has to be the pick of the round. The warning signs had been there for Kashima, but few would have picked Mito to take advantage – however Arata Wantanabe went on the rampage, scoring 4 times to send the 2025 J2 Champions into dreamland.

## DEFENSIVE ORGANISATION OF THE ROUND

Well, **JEF United Chiba**, it’s not half self inflicted sometimes. Imagine being stable at 1-1, looking forward to your first point, it’s the 93rd minute (of 95) and you have a free kick deep in your own half, your goalkeeper standing buy to hoy it downfield. How then, after TWO headers, are you suddenly facing a 2 v 1 break? The sight of  6 JEF United players desperately trying to catch Yuta Kamiya of Okayama is indicative of how their season has gone so far – a lot of effort, not much craft.
