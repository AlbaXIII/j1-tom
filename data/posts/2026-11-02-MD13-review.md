A new hand touches the beacon at the top of the table, one belonging to **FC Tokyo**. It took until first half stoppage time to break the deadlock away at  **Fagiano Okayama**, but once the dam broke it was a thoroughly dominant performance from the new leaders, who are now in very scary form - unbeaten since August, haven't conceded a goal since September, and the aggregate score of their last 5 games is 17-0. Flying under the radar as they have done, it looks like they will take some stopping. Stepping up to the challenge remain **Sanfrecce Hiroshima**, who disposed of **Mito Hollyhock** with regulation ease. After an early red card for the visitors Sanfrecce never looked likely to lose their grip on the game and ran out confortable 3-0 winners. Slipping off the top are **Machida Zelvia**, who welcomed a scrappy - though not quite resurgent just yet - **Gamba Osaka**, and a game that despite having enough chances for both sides to find a winner ended goalless. **Kashiwa Reysol** also stay within touching distance of the summit with a win over **Urawa Reds**, although the 4-0 scoreline flatters the hosts who were made to work hard for it by the visitors and had 2 second-half stoppage time strikes to add gloss to the scoreline - with Urawa reduced to 10 just before the final knockings of the game.

Just back from the top 4, **Vissel Kobe** got back to winning ways with a 3-1 win over **Nagoya Grampus**. Vissel went 0-1 down in the first 3 minutes before turning the game on its head in 10 minutes either side of half time. Nagoya were denied an equalizer by a very marginal VAR offside call before Ren Komatsu sealed it at the death. **Kashima Antlers** also bounced back to stay just about in touch with the leading pack, disposing of **Cerezo Osaka** in an impressive display of finishing, the scoreline ending at 4-1. A huge game looms for the holders - win away at FC Tokyo and they will feel confident that their early season blips can be left behind. **Kyoto Sanga** have had a brutal run of fixtures of late but got their first win in 5 against a disappointing **Shimizu S-Pulse**, Rafael Elias leading the charge with a brace to seal a 2-0 win.

In the basement battle, another huge result for **Tokyo Verdy**, building on their first home win of the season by traveling to **V-Varen Nagasaki** and coming away with their first 3 points on the road of the season, reacting well to being pegged back to win 1-2 in the last 10 minutes. The Green Machine are now, somehow, out of the relegation zone and have another monumental opportunity to put breathing space between them and the other strugglers next time away to Shimizu. 

## GAME(S) OF THE ROUND

For pure fun, look no further than **Yokohama F. Marinos** 4-2 **Kawasaki Frontale**. Marinos have been draw specialists of late but broke their streak of 4 stalemates in a row in some fashion, easing into a 4-1 lead in impressively dominant fashion, and indeed are currently looking strong in all areas ahead of their next crucial run - 2 of the top 4 in the next 4 games.

However for me it's got to be **JEF United Chiba** 3-0 **Avispa Fukuoka**. Despite the faintly "Doctor Tottenham" feeling to Avispa at the moment after they were beaten by Verdy last week JEF needed a performance to give them hope for the relegation dogfight and they rose to the occasion, controlling the game from the first whistle and leaving the visitors rooted to the bottom of the table, as the drop zone picture shifts again. Mito Hollyhock up next for JEF, win that and there will be real belief they can get out of the mire.

## GOAL OF THE ROUND

So many to choose from this week. **Dani Gomez** showed some real class with his two finishes against Okayama, and **Rafael Elias**' improvised volley opener against Shimizu was the inciting moment of quality for his team, whilst **Kenshin Yuba** finessed a beauty from outside the box against Urawa.

Just pipping them to the post this week is **Akito Suzuki** of Sanfrecce Hiroshima, with an outstanding piece of finishing (not to mention athleticism) to volley home the opener against Mito.
