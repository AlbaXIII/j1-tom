Matchday 6 comes around. and the story of the season is beginning to take shape. We begin at the top of the table, where early frontrunners Kashima Antlers and Kashiwa Reysol both continued their dips in form - with Kashima coming unstuck 0-1 at home to a resurgent Urawa (3 on the bounce for the Red Diamonds now), whilst Kashiwa went one worse by losing 0-2 on their own patch to Yokohama F. Marinos despite starting well. This left the door open for Machida Zelvia to capitalise, and they duly obliged, securing a 1-3 win away at Nagoya to go 3 points clear in a game featuring 4 of the goofiest goals you will see in any one match. Vissel Kobe have fully recovered from a slow start and are now 3 wins on the bounce after turning V-Varen Nagasaki over 3-1, whilst FC Tokyo seem to have put their indifferent start behind them with another regulation home win over Kyoto Sanga to make it 5 unbeaten for the Gas. Mito Hollyhock continued their strong form, holding Avispa Fukuoka to a 1-1 draw, whilst Cerezo Osaka and Tokyo Verdy did their level best to provide a cure for insomnia by drawing 0-0 in a game that can most charitably be described as 'it happened'.

<br>

## GAME OF THE ROUND

All this leaves 3 competitors for GotW. Fagiano Okayama overcame Sanfrecce Hiroshima 3-2 in a seesaw contest that saw the home side see out the final half an hour with 10 men in a heroic rearguard action. Kawasaki Frontale and Shimizu S-Pulse played out a bad-tempered, chaotic, and hugely entertaining 3-1 home win, with many highlights (and many many lowlights), but the gong this week goes to **JEF United Chiba 2-0 Gamba Osaka**. First points, win, and clean sheet of the season for the newly promoted _inu_ at the 6th time of asking, and they thoroughly deserved it against a consistently inconsistent Gamba, who missed talisman Takashi Usami.

## PLAYER OF THE ROUND

As with last week, not sure you can score a hat-trick and not get player of the round. Okayama's **Kota Kanawo** the recipient this week, ably assisted by strike partner Lucão.
