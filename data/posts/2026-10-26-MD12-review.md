After the midweek madness it was back to normality for the J League this weekend, and it was a matchday of late drama, huge wins and even more movement in the table. The title picture remains tight, the relegation picture no clearer, and the mid-table picture swirls and shifts. 

<br>

Unusually we're going to start at the bottom this week, and that is because - wait for it - **Tokyo Verdy** have finally won a league match. The victims were visitors **Avsipa Fukuoka**, who did peg Verdy back after going a goal down but fell to two goals in the last 16 minutes from Yuan Matsuhashi and Issei Kumatoriya to slip to a 3-1 loss. For Avispa, the result is disastrous, as they slip below Verdy in the table to 19th and are rooted in the relegation mire. The signs of improvement for Verdy had been evident since new manager Naoki Soma took over a few weeks ago, despite results, but the draw in midweek and this massive win means there might still be hope for the capital city side yet. Returning to matters of the title-chasers, **Vissel Kobe** and **Machida Zelvia** played out a 0-0 draw in Kobe in what was billed the game of the weekend - both sides cancelling each other out despite Machida having the better of the openings, and it is they who remain at the summit, but now only by a single goal. Elswhere in the chasing pack it was another regulation home win for **Sanfrecce Hiroshima** who bounced back from their surprise midweek loss at Nagasaki to convincingly turn over **Shimizu S-Pulse** 4-0. 

Threatening to crash the title aspirant party are the resurgent pair of **Kawasaki Frontale** & **Urawa Reds**, who are up to 6th and 7th respectively on the back of 3 straight wins. There was a huge Brazilian influence to both matches as Frontale saw off **Fagiano Okayama** in a 2-1 win marked by Marcinho's 8th of the season, whilst Urawa talisman Matheus Sávio scored the only goal of the game from the visit of **V-Varen Nagasaki**.

In mid-table, **Gamba Osaka** battled to a 2-2 draw against visitors **Kyoto Sanga** despite losing keeper Jun Ichimori to a red card before the hour mark, and indeed will be kicking themselves to not go away with all 3 points - a 92nd minute equaliser from Takumi Kato stealing the draw for Kyoto. Draw specialists **Yokohama F. Marinos** stayed true to form in their trip to **Nagoya Grampus**, a 1-1 draw making it 4 stalemates in a row. 

<br>

## GAME(S) OF THE ROUND

Breaking with tradition this week, there are 3 games of the weekend (stop it J league, you're spoiling us).

Firstly, in the title race, was a huge clash between 2 of the pre-season favourites and dyslexics nightmare as **Kashima Antlers** welcomed **Kashiwa Reysol**. Kashima have been listing badly of late, whilst Kashiwa were coming off a shock defeat at home to Nagoya last weekend and being held by FC Tokyo in midweek. In somewhat of a running theme for Reysol games this year, they went behind early, but came roaring back with 3 unanswered goals to stun Kashima - who could only respond with Aleksandar Čavrić too late to leave them falling to a 2-3 defeat. Another convincing win against a title rival from Reysol, who have beaten Vissel Kobe, Machida Zelvia and now Kashima from their title rivals. If they can sort out their erratic form against the teams below them, they are surely going to take this race the whole way. Kashima on the other hand slip further behind to 8th, and 7 points off the top. Léo Ceará cannot return soon enough.

## SAVE OF THE ROUND

Shimizu S-Pulse forwards Ryo Germain returned to former club Sanfrecce Hiroshima this weekend clearly with a point to prove, and when he broke through in the 56th minute, chopped back onto his right foot, and looked the keeper in the eye, it looked all the world like a goal. But once again **Keisuke Osaka** said no. Getting down to his left to turn the ball round the post in a feat of supreme reflexes to leave Germain crestfallen, and his team well beaten.
