Mini-review this week because shift work meant I had zero time - but the J League never sleeps.

More twists and turns in the title race left **Machida Zelvia** clear of the chasing pack, with **Kashiwa Reysol**, **Sanfrecce Hiroshima**, **FC Tokyo**, **Vissel Kobe** & **Kashima Antlers** all slipping up. At the bottom of the table the clear winners were **Gamba Osaka**, who broke a long winless drought with victory in a crunch match against **Avispa Fukuoka**.

<br>

## GAME(S) OF THE ROUND

Call me biased but my game of this midweek round is **Tokyo Verdy** coming back from 2-0 down in the last quarter of the game to snatch a point at **Yokohama F. Marinos**. Perhaps there is hope for Verdy yet, however faint.
