J1-Tom - The home of English-language Japanese Football.

J1 League - the highest level of football in Japan

Tomodachi (ともだち) -friend

In August 2023, I finally managed to go on a bucket list trip to Japan.

As a lifelong football fan, I managed to make my way down to a J-League game whilst staying with my cousin in Osaka.

I've been lucky enough in my personal and work life to travel to many different countries, and sample the beautiful game all over the world.

The J League experience was one that instantly drew me in. The fan culture, the stadium experience, and the game itself left me wanting to explore further.

Since that day, I have managed to visit Japan on a further two occasions, and of course have made time to check the fixture list before going!

J1-Tom is my attempt to create a simple English-language portal for those, like me, who want an easy way to learn more about the beautiful game in Japan.
