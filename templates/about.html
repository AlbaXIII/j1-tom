J1-Tom - The home of English-language Japanese Football.

J1 League - the highest level of football in Japan

Tomodachi (ともだち) -friend

In August 2023, I finally managed to go on a bucket list trip to Japan for my honeymoon. As a lifelong football fan, I managed to talk my way into being able to head down to a J League game whilst staying with my cousin in Osaka. I didn't know much about the sport in Japan - I knew about the most famous exports, Hidetoshi Nakata and Shinsuke Nakamura in the 2000s, Kaoru Mitoma today, eternal round of 16 heartbreak at every World Cup - but in terms of how the game was consumed in Japan, I had no idea.

Now I've been lucky enough in my personal and work life to travel to many different countries, and sample the beautiful game all over the world. It used to be my job at the start of my career (in a roundabout way), and I've probably been able to watch football in more countries than the average fan. What I saw that sweltering evening in Osaka left an impression that will last forever. The J League experience was one that instantly drew me in. The passion of the fans, the accessibility of the grounds, the interaction between team and the supporters, and the match-day 'event' were absolutely top drawer. Not to mention it was really cheap!

Since then, I've been able to get back to Japan a couple more times and I've alway made time to check out the local J League team, and never once have I regretted it. However, it's not a particularly easy league to follow in Europe. So J1-Tom is my attempt to combing my affection for Japanese football with my coding & writing hobbies, to make a casual place for curious fans to check out a hidden gem of a league.

NB - I am a hobbyist and not associated with the J1 League in any way (yet. Call me fellas.), and my opinions are mostly wrong and my own.
